## Title
Silk Touch Spawners: Pick Up and Move Mob Spawners

## Short description
Mine a mob spawner with Silk Touch to pick it up, then place it anywhere. It keeps its mob and settings.

## Introduction
Found a perfect spawner in the wrong place? Mine it with a Silk Touch pickaxe and take it with you. Place it at your farm and it works exactly like before: same mob, same settings. No experiments needed.

## Description
- Mine a spawner with a **Silk Touch pickaxe** and it drops as a **Mob Spawner** item.
- Place the item anywhere to get the same spawner back, with the same mob and settings.
- Picking one up costs your pickaxe one point of durability. Unbreaking works as normal.
- Without Silk Touch, spawners break like vanilla.
- In Creative, the item isn't used up, so you can place copies.

Good to know:
- The item doesn't show which mob is inside, so keep track of which is which.
- Spawners can't be placed in Adventure mode.

## Installation
1. Download the .mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports both packs.
2. Create or edit a world and turn on the Silk Touch Spawners behavior pack (the resource pack is added with it). No experiments needed.

## Supported Minecraft versions
1.26.60 and newer
